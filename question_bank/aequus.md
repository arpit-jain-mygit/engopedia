# Question bank: root "aequus" (equal)

Source: Word Power Made Easy workbook, 19 words built from aequus.
Status: DRAFT for review. Not loaded into the app.

Format: question, options A-D, correct answer, why it's right (and why the trap is wrong).

---

**Q0. Root check**
What does the root *aequus* mean?
A. Equal ✅  B. Voice  C. Night  D. Balance
*Why:* aequus = equal. Voice is vox, night is nox, balance is libra.

**Q1. equivocate**
When asked if the project would finish on time, the manager said, "Well, that depends on many things," and never gave a clear answer. What did the manager do?
A. Equivocated ✅  B. Equated  C. Equalized  D. Equilibrated
*Why:* equi (equal) + voc (voice): speak so the words could mean two things. Equate and equalize mean "make equal"; equilibrate is not a word in this bank.

**Q2. equality**
Under the law, ___ means every citizen has the same rights, whatever their background.
A. Equanimity  B. Equality ✅  C. Equinox  D. Equilibrium
*Why:* equality is the state of being equal. Equanimity is calm, equinox is equal day and night, equilibrium is balance.

**Q3. equivocation**
"I neither confirm nor deny the report, and I'm not saying it's false." This statement is an example of:
A. Equilateral  B. Equity  C. Equivocation ✅  D. Equinoctial
*Why:* a deliberately vague statement that avoids a clear yes or no.

**Q4. equivocal**
The mayor gave an ___ answer, so the reporters were still confused.
A. Unequivocal  B. Equivocal ✅  C. Equable  D. Equilateral
*Why:* equivocal = open to two meanings. Unequivocal (the trap) means clear with one meaning, the opposite.

**Q5. unequivocal**
The witness said, "Yes, I saw him at nine o'clock," leaving no room for doubt. The answer was:
A. Equivocal  B. Equitable  C. Unequivocal ✅  D. Equinoctial
*Why:* un- (not) + equivocal. One clear meaning only.

**Q6. equity**
Two runners start a race, but one gets a head start to make up for a slower leg. Which word describes this kind of fairness?
A. Equality  B. Equity ✅  C. Equanimity  D. Equinox
*Why:* equity means fairness that adjusts for different needs. Equality (the trap) gives everyone the same treatment.

**Q7. equitable**
A rule that gives students with a disability extra time is best described as:
A. Equilateral  B. Equitable ✅  C. Equable  D. Equivocal
*Why:* equitable = fair and just to all. Equable (the trap) means calm in temper.

**Q8. inequity**
A rule that pays men more than women for the same job shows an ___ in pay.
A. Equity  B. Equinox  C. Inequity ✅  D. Equilibrium
*Why:* in- (not) + equity. The unfairness itself.

**Q9. inequitable**
Which word describes a rule that treats students unfairly because of where they come from?
A. Equitable  B. Equable  C. Inequitable ✅  D. Equilateral
*Why:* unfair or unjust. Equitable is the opposite.

**Q10. iniquity**
The villain's ___ shocked the whole town: he had poisoned the town well.
A. Equity  B. Iniquity ✅  C. Equilibrium  D. Equanimity
*Why:* iniquity = wickedness, a much stronger word than inequity. Only the spelling is similar.

**Q11. iniquitous**
The ___ scheme cheated poor families out of their homes.
A. Equitable  B. Iniquitous ✅  C. Equable  D. Unequivocal
*Why:* iniquitous = wicked, sinful.

**Q12. equinox**
On the day of the spring ___, day and night last about the same length.
A. Equinox ✅  B. Equivocation  C. Equanimity  D. Equilateral
*Why:* equinox = equal night. Only the equinox is a time of year.

**Q13. equinoctial**
Which word describes storms that happen around the time of the equinox?
A. Equinoctial ✅  B. Equilateral  C. Equable  D. Equity
*Why:* equinoctial = relating to the equinox.

**Q14. equanimity**
Even after losing the final, she accepted the result with calm and composure. Which word fits?
A. Equanimity ✅  B. Equivocation  C. Equinox  D. Equilibrium
*Why:* equanimity = calm, even temper under stress.

**Q15. equability**
Which word describes a steady, even-tempered calm that makes someone easy to work with?
A. Equability ✅  B. Equilibrium  C. Equilateral  D. Equivocation
*Why:* equability = evenness of temper. Equilibrium is balance of forces, not temper.

**Q16. equable**
He stayed ___ even when the crowd shouted at him.
A. Equable ✅  B. Equilateral  C. Equinox  D. Iniquitous
*Why:* equable = calm, unruffled.

**Q17. equilibrium**
A tightrope walker keeps her body in ___ by moving her arms.
A. Equilibrium ✅  B. Equanimity  C. Equity  D. Equinox
*Why:* equilibrium = balance between opposing forces.

**Q18. equilibrist**
A tightrope walker in the circus is called an:
A. Equilibrist ✅  B. Equinoctial  C. Equanimity  D. Equity
*Why:* equilibrist = a performer who keeps balance.

**Q19. equilateral**
A triangle with all three sides the same length is called:
A. Equilibrium  B. Equivocal  C. Equinox  D. Equilateral ✅
*Why:* equi (equal) + latus (side): equal sides.
